The HUB exists because isolated repos, SoundCloud uploads, and sample packs do not compound unless they route into each other. This site makes the chain explicit.

Production stance:
This public repo does not pretend checkout, cloud accounts, or signed installers are live. It presents the current ecosystem honestly while making the next click obvious: download a plugin, hear the catalog, inspect release proof, or follow the platform roadmap.
