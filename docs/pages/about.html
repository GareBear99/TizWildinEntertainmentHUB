About the HUB

Free audio plugins, release routes, and crawlable proof in one verified HUB.

The TizWildin Entertainment HUB is the public front door for the creator ecosystem built by Gary Richard Doman (GareBear99 / TizWildin), an independent developer and producer based in Williams Lake, British Columbia, Canada.

It exists to solve a simple problem: Gary's work was scattered across dozens of repositories, itch.io listings, sample packs, articles and tools. The HUB pulls them into one place where a producer can find a free plugin, check for updates, grab a sample pack, read the research behind it, and follow the release — with every claim traceable to a repo, a release, or a manifest.

What lives here

  • FreeEQ8 — the flagship: a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) 8-band parametric EQ for macOS, Windows and Linux (VST3 / AU / Standalone), with dynamic EQ, linear phase, match EQ and per-band drive. ProEQ8 is the $20, 24-band commercial tier.
  • FreeVox8 — a JUCE spectral vocoder with ghost-resynthesis and morph modes.
  • The Maid Suite, Therum, RiftWave, Instrudio, XyloCore and more — 20 free plugins in total, mostly built with JUCE.
  • 11 free sample packs — 808s, risers, dark piano, violin, phonk, future bass and the TizWildin-branded kits (Aurora, Chime, Chroma, Obsidian, Skyline).
  • Browser tools — playable Instrudio violin, bongo and harp, Voxel Audio (RGB waveform visualizer) and BannerBass.
  • Games & engines — Neolution and RiftAscent, plus itch.io game assets and engines from GareBearProductionz.
  • Research — the FreeEQ8/ProEQ8 DAFx26 paper and technical articles on DSP, ARC and local AI.
  • ARC source spine — the local-first AI and authority/receipts backbone (ARC-Core, ARC-Neuron, Lucifer runtime, Arc-RAR, OmniBinary).

How money works here

The HUB itself never takes payment. Every product has a free version; upgrades live inside each product (ProEQ8 licences, Master Key seats), and itch.io assets check out on itch.io. The HUB handles discovery, update checks, downloads and release proof — not checkout. There are no accounts required to browse, and no paywall on any free download.

How to verify anything on this site

Every item in the catalogue has a public route page (see the Ecosystem Index), a machine-readable record in public-index.json, and a sitemap entry. Plugin releases are checked live against GitHub. If a claim on this site cannot be traced to a repo, a release, or a manifest, it should not be there — tell us.

Who runs it

One person: Gary Doman. The HUB is a personal project, not a company. Support, licensing questions and bug reports go through the routes on the Contact page.
